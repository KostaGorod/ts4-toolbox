# Llama Plumbob Glitch Fixer — Design Context

Source: `/shape` session, brief confirmed.

## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences, one landing page:

1. **Hero: annoyed Sims player** whose custom loading screen is visibly glitching — the plumbob sprite keeps swapping frames with a llama head mid-spin. They Google'd "Sims 4 llama plumbob glitch" or "loading screen plumbob flickering" and clicked the first link that looked credible. Do NOT describe their state as "game won't load" or "stuck on loading screen" — that overclaims the bug's severity and will make accurate Simmers distrust the tool. They are not technical, they do not read long pages, and they need "drop the broken file → download a working one" to be instantly comprehensible.
2. **Secondary: creative fan** who wants to put their own art on the Sims 4 loading screen. They arrive browsing, not panicked. They want a small creative surface and enough trust to hand it an image.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **playful, insider-warm, plainly honest.**

- Voice is one Simmer helping another, not a product speaking to a market.
- Copy can say "plumbob", "llama", "The Sims 4" freely. Affectionate fan-community register, not marketing-speak.
- No humor in error messages; humor is reserved for celebrations.
- Jargon is avoided except where the audience uses the jargon themselves ("`.package`", "instance ID" when relevant).

### Aesthetic Direction
Playful fan-zine warmth. A tool that looks like a Simmer made it for other Simmers, not a SaaS product.

- **Theme**: light. A stressed user on a phone at 11pm needs warm-paper calm, not a dark-mode neon devtool.
- **Palette** (OKLCH, all deliberately off-Sims):
  - Background: warm cream `oklch(96% 0.018 75)`
  - Surface / card: `oklch(93% 0.025 70)`
  - Ink (text): deep navy-ink `oklch(24% 0.03 260)`
  - Ink-muted: `oklch(52% 0.04 60)` (warm muted gray, tinted toward the cream)
  - **Primary (Fix CTA)**: coral `oklch(68% 0.17 27)` — deliberately not green, so there is zero color-level echo of plumbob-green
  - **Secondary (Make CTA)**: buttery gold `oklch(78% 0.14 78)`
  - Success: same buttery gold
  - Error: terracotta `oklch(58% 0.14 28)`
- **Type**: Fredoka (display) + Hanken Grotesk (body). Neither is in impeccable's reflex-reject list. Fredoka carries the playful-warm register in headlines; Hanken Grotesk keeps body text legible and modern without reading as "AI default".
- **Shapes**: chunky radii (12–28px). No Material shadows — warm outline/tint depth instead.
- **Illustration**: original hand-authored SVG of a stylized *stuck house with a door mid-open*, cream-and-coral. Serves as the hero mascot. Explicitly no diamond shapes, no llama imagery, no EA-derivative elements anywhere visually.

### Identity
- Wordmark: **Llama Plumbob Glitch Fixer**
- Tag/subtitle: *Sims 4 Loading Screen Fixer*
- Explicit fan-tool disclaimer in footer: "Not affiliated with Electronic Arts or Maxis. *The Sims 4* is a trademark of Electronic Arts Inc."

### Design Principles
1. **Fix is the headliner.** Layout, size, and color weight all privilege the Fix flow. Make is a co-citizen below, not a collapsed `<details>`.
2. **Warm before clever.** Every surface has to feel helpful first. Puns are additive, never load-bearing.
3. **No EA visual footprint.** Words are fair-use, shapes and palette are original. No green-diamond silhouettes, no llama art, no game UI mimicry.
4. **Honest about the bug.** Copy names the *visible symptom* ("plumbob flickering into a llama mid-spin"). Don't overclaim into "game hangs", "never finishes loading", "stuck on loading screen" — those are different, more severe failure modes this tool doesn't necessarily fix. The bug this tool addresses is the flicker itself.
5. **Playful celebration, plain errors.** Success states can be cheeky; error states follow the what/why/how-to-fix formula with zero jokes.
6. **Accessibility is baseline, not an afterthought.** WCAG AA contrast minimum, keyboard-reachable hero and queue, `prefers-reduced-motion` disables mascot animation and dragover glow.

### Technical Constraints
- Static page, single bundle, no backend at runtime.
- Bundle budget: ≤ 400 KB total.
- Self-hosted fonts (no Google Fonts fetch).
- Font-display: swap with size-adjusted fallbacks to minimize layout shift.
- Mobile-first; top nav collapses to wordmark + star button under 640 px.
